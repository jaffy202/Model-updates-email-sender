
Model: ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview
Lead: ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview is a language model from Baidu, released in November 2026, with a 1.0M-token context window, and pricing from $0.280/M input, $0.028/M cached input, $1.13/M output.
Description: ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview is Baidu's first post-trained model built on ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base. It is a mixture-of-experts model with 280 billion total parameters and 14 billion active parameters, a 1,048,576-token context window, and text-only input and output. Baidu describes it as strong at coding, 3D modeling, knowledge work, and content creation, and as an intermediate post-training checkpoint: some capabilities may still fall short, and an official (non-preview) release is planned. The API is available on the Qianfan platform as ernie-5.5-flash-preview. Baidu says the weights will be open-sourced under Apache 2.0. Qianfan international pay-as-you-go pricing is $0.28 per million input tokens, $0.028 per million cache-hit tokens, and $1.13 per million output tokens. On Qianfan, thinking cannot be disabled, and reasoning effort cannot be adjusted (the documented default is high).

Lead: Qwen4-Max-Preview is a language model from Qwen, released in November 2026, with multimodal input.
Description: Qwen4-Max-Preview is the next-generation flagship multimodal model in the Qwen family, combining text generation, image and video understanding, and deep reasoning for coding, office productivity, frontend design, and complex agentic tasks. Self-reported scores (safeguards off) include Terminal-Bench 4.0 70.5%, Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 75.3% and de novo protein binder design 85.1%. Available as `claude-mythos-5-2` on the Claude API, Claude Models on Google Cloud and Microsoft Foundry, and as `anthropic.claude-mythos-5-2` on Amazon Bedrock, where the Cyber Verification Program is currently open only to organizations with a data retention exemption for Fable 5.2 or Fable 5.1.

Model: SWE-2 Flash
Lead: SWE-2 Flash is a language model from Cognition, released in November 2026.
Description: SWE-2 Flash is Cognition's fast, low-cost coding model, built on Thinking Machines' Inkling-Small (276B total / 12B active parameters) with a large-scale mid-training stage (a few trillion tokens of scientific articles, blogs, code and other sources, in three stages) followed by large-scale reinforcement learning on higher-quality, more diverse task types. Cognition reports 24.0% on FrontierCode 2.0, 45.5% on FrontierCode 1.1 Main, 60.1% on DeepSWE 1.1, 20.0% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and 89.2% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, and states it outperforms all American open-weight models. Training targeted cybersecurity work (ExploitBench 34.8%, up from 11.3% for the base model). It is available starting 2026-11-04 in Devin Desktop and Devin CLI (free through 2026-11-30) at one-fifth the price and about 3x the generation speed (roughly 200 vs 60 tokens/s) of SWE-2. Weights are not public; Cognition plans a program to share them with trusted industry partners and researchers. No standalone public API, per-token pricing, or context-window specification has been published.
